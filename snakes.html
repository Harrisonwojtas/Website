Reptiles and Clay

2026 has been my year of the snake. Pulled from clay and scaled with precise sculpting pokers. Often referencing real species of the 3,900 species of snakes across the earth.

My Master piece. The snake is modeled off the Emerald Tree boa. Will he consume its world? Where will he go once the vase is swallowed?

More about snakes.

Flakes and Snakes

We'd often joke when friends would bail on meeting up at parties or going on hikes. "Snakes and flakes," we would hiss at one another, often followed by an eye roll or a well-timed "Huuuuuh?" The snake represents cunning and sneaking around — someone you distrust, unbeknownst to you, out to get you.
