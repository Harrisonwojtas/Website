Reptiles and Clay

2026 has been my year of the snake. Pulled from clay and scaled with precise sculpting Pokers. Often refrerencing real species of the 3,900 species of snakes across the earth.

Flakes and Snakes

We'd often joke whne friends would bail on meetin up at parties or going on hikes. "Snakes and Flakes" we would hiss at one and other, often followed by an eye roll or a well timed "Huuuuuuh?". The snake is representing the cunning and sneaks. Someone who you distrust and are unbenownst out to get you.
