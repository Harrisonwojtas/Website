Welcome to my art website!

My name is Harry. I am a ceramicist, artist, scientist, based in Sitka Alaska. When I'm not working in the lab, I try to spend my time carving blocks, throwing pots, or taking photos. This is a place I can display my current and past works for y'all.

Updates

I am pursuing my masters degree in Vancouver WA and have continued to pursue ceramic projects. Snakes and more sculptures are on my mind. Wheel thrown vessels and such.

I believe in keeping things simple and letting the work speak for itself. Thanks for stopping by!
